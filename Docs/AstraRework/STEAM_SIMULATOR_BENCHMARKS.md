# Steam simulator design benchmarks

Research date: 6 September 2026. Five official Steam pages and three published screenshots per game were inspected. These are design references, not evidence of measured performance or player experience. Local source URLs/copies are indexed in `Geode/Output/AstraResearch/sources.json`; no third-party game imagery is a production asset.

| Reference / observed evidence | Original Geode design consequence | Do not copy |
|---|---|---|
| **Supermarket Simulator** connects computer ordering, stocking, cash/card checkout and expansion. Its screenshots make the carried box, destination shelf and prices readable; checkout has a prominent amount display and a physical customer/payment position. [Official page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2670630/Supermarket_Simulator/) | Give each specimen a visible journey: delivery → work → sale display → customer → checkout. Put management beside real work and make the destination legible while carrying. Reserve staff access behind checkout. | Exact shop plan, shelf assets, characters or POS interface. Geode should concentrate more attention on the specimen than repeated shelf stocking. |
| **TCG Card Shop Simulator** combines selling stock with keeping valuable discoveries. Store images show an entrance/open sign, street connection, individually presented stock, customers and space between fixtures. Pack reveals focus on the discovery. [Official page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3070070/TCG_Card_Shop_Simulator/) | Keep collection distinct from retail ownership. Present a real find closely, then let the player decide its future. Show arrivals through the street door and growth through earned stock density. | Cards, branding, event systems, large arcade reveal overlays or neon rarity effects. Published future release plans do not establish that Early Access has already ended. |
| **Gas Station Simulator** frames renovation and expansion through a recognizable roadside building. Inspected exterior views use worn materials, practical structures and a visible horizon; two are cinematic dusk marketing views. [Official page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1149620/Gas_Station_Simulator/) | Give the mineral shop a clear frontage, real threshold and useful infrastructure. Wear should follow use. A bounded pavement/street view establishes place without an open world. | Its desert setting, building silhouette, exact equipment or cinematic darkness. A beauty image does not prove usable interaction exposure. |
| **Car Mechanic Simulator 2021** shows distinct repair areas and detailed machinery at working distance. Garage shots use material changes, lifting equipment and localized task prompts. One fluid image has a floating bottle; an inspection view ghosts bodywork. [Official page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1190000/Car_Mechanic_Simulator_2021/) | Make the cracker, saw and lap recognizable by working mechanism and contact. Stage controls within reach and preserve specimen identity through processing. | Floating tools, x-ray presentation, branded components or unrelated disassembly systems. |
| **King of Retail** screenshots show grouped products, varied fixture heights and busy customer/checkout areas. Its management premise includes organizing a growing store. The reviewed corporate map is outside this project's scope. [Official page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/968250/King_of_Retail/) | Group minerals and fixtures so the scene reads as an operating business. Keep a separate staff route and low sightlines through the showroom center. | Corporate expansion, deep employee management, oversaturated flooring, crowded labels or its exact store/UI layout. |

The following are Geode design decisions inferred from the references and master specification. Dimensions are proposed Geode budgets, not measurements of the comparison games.

| Pattern | Geode requirement and assessment |
|---|---|
| Storefront, entrance and exit | A first-person arrival view identifies the shop, door and opening state. Customers cross the actual threshold from a visible arrival route and leave believably. No spawn in the workroom. |
| OPEN/CLOSED and arrivals | One sign/UI action controls admission. Closing admits no new customers while the existing queue finishes. Sign, UI and saved state agree. |
| Aisles, checkout and browsing | Reserve a continuous 1.2 m public route where possible, clear checkout approach and separate cashier entrance. Measure actual colliders, queue envelopes and reach positions; nominal area is insufficient. |
| Management workstation | A modest 13–14 inch laptop belongs to the starter checkout kit. Inventory, orders, costs and physical consequences remain readable with keyboard/mouse and controller. |
| Workstation zoning | Wet washing/cutting belongs beside plumbing; dry cracking/inspection stays clear of water. Each moving mechanism and operator area has a measured envelope. Receiving never occupies the cracking approach. |
| Tiny-to-mature progression | Day 1 has checkout/laptop, basic cracking and finite receiving. Bought, delivered and placed equipment produces visible change; leases open connected space with operating costs. |
| Density and environmental storytelling | Useful props cluster on work surfaces and edges. Stock and wear follow use. Empty early shelves are honest; mature density comes from owned specimens and earned equipment. |
| Lighting and materials | Neutral storefront light preserves mineral colour. Pale plaster, concrete, blue steel, cream enamel and oak separate surfaces. Limited warm task pools avoid an orange room-wide cast. |
| UI and upgrades | Lead with image, item, cost, prerequisite, capacity, destination and running cost. Show real owned thumbnails, exact checkout cents and obvious controller focus. Reject placeholder dots, giant slabs and unsupported statistics. |
| Interaction feedback and tutorial | Keep the contact point visible. One contextual objective accompanies a real runtime target; avoid stacked overlays. Discovery follows the physical reveal promptly. |
| Sound and ambience | Shop state, exterior, customers and powered equipment should be audible at meaningful positions. Use quiet space and dynamic range. Direct listening in Geode must judge quality; still images cannot. |
| Frame pacing and scale | The master requires smooth 60 FPS. Measure standalone CPU/GPU, percentiles, GC, generation and populated-room costs. Reviews, screenshots and trailers cannot establish these figures. |

The study supports the original concepts in [ART_DIRECTION.md](ART_DIRECTION.md), including their corrections. It does not certify visual parity, audio, accessibility, persistence, sales success or Steam readiness. Those remain playable-game gates.
